## Verdicts (locked)

### 1. Legacies: memorial garden, NO buffs
The grave plot grows into a **memorial garden** — each ascended spirit adds a named stone; the garden becomes the farm's heart and record. **No mechanical buffs** from legacies (overrides the skeleton slice doc's shrine-buff idea). The reward is the place, the names, and the journal gallery.

### 2. Final wishes: one fixed wish per species
Each species always asks the same authored thing. Wishes become species lore. Cheap at 50-species scale; written in the dark-funny-kind tone register.

### 3. Ascension site is a buildable
You **build the ascension spot when you unlock the moment** — place it where it feels right and decorate around it. The site is a customizable landmark, not a fixed altar. (Replaces the bootstrapped altar placement; altar becomes a build-menu structure gated on first fulfilment.)

### 4. Ceremony: THE STYX CROSSING (replaces the light column — owner, verbatim intent)
At the built ascension pad, when the player confirms:
1. The screen **darkens all around** except the spirits gathered around you and the pad — an eerie but hopeful gleam of the amassed Spirit.
2. **A river flows in** with **Charon** on it, who asks for payment.
3. The departing spirit **gleams out its spirit orbs** to him — its amassed Spirit IS the passage coin.
4. It **boards the boat and they sail off** into the dark.
5. Lighting returns to normal, and **the headstone drops** for you to pick up and place (feeds the memorial garden).
Nearby residents gather and watch (farewell chirp chorus). The river comes to you — no walk to a riverbank. This recasts Charon as the figure at the heart of the whole fiction -- and he appears NOWHERE else (owner rule 2026-10-01: not a vendor, not the land office).

### 5. NO anti-hoarding push
No whispers, no plateaus, no decay. Ascension happens **when the player feels it's time** — attachment is valid play (the Pokemon starter principle: some never pull them out, and that's theirs). If the legacy pull isn't enough, the answer is making the garden more beautiful, never pressure.
