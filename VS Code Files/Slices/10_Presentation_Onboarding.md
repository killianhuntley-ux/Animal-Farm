# 10 — Presentation & Onboarding ✂ MILESTONE 3: DEMO

**Question this slice answers:** *Can a stranger with no instructions fall for this game in ten minutes — and does it finally look and sound like itself?*
**GDD:** §4 (all), §6.2 (never block on final art — this is where art arrives)

---

## Builds (systems & passes)

### A. Art integration pass
- Friend-artist skins land on the existing rigs: shepherd, the Prairie/Forest species set, Repo-man, vendors, cryptids (GDD §4.1 — the rig investment pays off here; animation untouched).
- Terrain/UI visual pass to the cartoon dark-comic register; **barren→lush** gets its real palette (GDD §4.1).
- Ghost silhouettes get their final eerie-cute treatment — this is the marketing image; make it screenshot-worthy.

### B. Audio pass (GDD §4.2, locked model)
- Musician-friend deliverables: 2 region ambient beds (day/night variants) · **Repo-man leitmotif** (the alarm) · Ascension + Weave ceremony themes · occasional low ambient tracks (Minecraft cadence — silence is the default).
- Human-gibberish voice sessions per species (friends + a mic + an afternoon; research: cheap, funny, zero localisation).

### C. Onboarding (GDD §4.4, all commitments)
- **The Guide:** disembodied light — teaches by *pointing* (drifts to the relevant thing, glows), minimal text, always honest.
- First-ten-minutes script: wake on empty land → Guide points at soil → first plant → **first silhouette appears ~minute 6–8** → first lure lands inside minute 10–15.
- Just-in-time triggers for every later system (first fulfilment, first weave hint, Repo-man's warning visit already handles himself). **No interruption cascades, no control theft beyond seconds** (VP's four failures inverted — research §6.4).
- Skippable: a "seasoned shepherd" toggle collapses the script to system-unlock notes.
- Gentle Passage (easy mode) offered at save creation, plainly described.

### D. Demo packaging
- Menu/title, settings (audio, controls remap, screen), credits, save-slot UI, build pipeline, versioned demo cut (content-fenced: Prairie + Forest, all systems).

## Content

Final-pass skins/audio as above · Guide VFX + ~30 short teaching lines (tone: warm, wry) · title screen art.

## Out of scope

Steam page/store assets (EA prep), localisation, achievements, trailer.

## Depends on

Everything 01–09 — this slice dresses the finished skeleton.

## Definition of Done — Demo acceptance

- [ ] **The stranger test:** someone who has never seen the game lures their first spirit in ≤15 minutes, unassisted, and can say what they want to do next
- [ ] Nobody reads a text box longer than two sentences in the first hour
- [ ] Art: a single screenshot communicates "cosy but something's off (in a fun way)" — the CotL positioning test (GDD §1.5)
- [ ] Audio: a full session has real silence; the leitmotif alarm works with eyes closed; at least one gibberish voice makes someone laugh
- [ ] Skip toggle verified by a VP-literate tester ("it got out of my way")
- [ ] ✂ Demo build distributable to a trusted circle beyond friends

## Risks

- **Friend-collaborator scheduling is the critical path** (GDD §6.2) — brief artists/musicians at slice 07–08, not at this slice's start; their lead time overlaps your systems work.
- Onboarding is playtested, not designed — budget multiple stranger tests with revisions between.
- Scope gravity: "presentation pass" invites infinite polish. The DoD list is the fence.
